# Phase101 — Original option BUY order/trade-ID provenance (no P&L or live activation)

**Stacked DRAFT on Phase100 PR #105. Future LIVE NRML positional CE/PE 1W/2W/4W/8W is the product objective, NOT yet authorised.** Phase90 real NRML BUY routing remains OFF, existing MIS-only 15:15 square-off preserved, and NRML positional holdings must never get an automatic 15:15 exit.

## Why this phase

Phase99 stores immutable BROKER-REPORTED SELL child and per-execution trade IDs. Phase100's price-difference preview used the original BUY attempt's persisted filled quantity and average fill price, but those numbers are **not independently matched to individual original BUY exchange executions**. A stale or overwritten order summary, duplicated BUY trade ID, mismatched product/contract, wrong venue reference, or incomplete original fill could make even an *indicative* result misleading. Neither current brokerage account state nor today's order-book GET can guarantee historical completeness.

Upstox officially documents the `GET /v2/order/trades?order_id=...` fields `trade_id`, `order_id`, `exchange_order_id`, `instrument_token`, `product`, `transaction_type`, `quantity`, and `average_price` (Upstox developer docs, Get Order Trades). These broker-source fields are **not signed independent venue receipts**.

## Implementation

- Pure `adaptiveDurableBuyTradeEvidence.ts` validates the original requested whole-lot BUY, exact durable position/attempt/account, requested native instrument/product D (NRML) or I (MIS), broker order ID, broker-reported exchange order ID and `COMPLETE` child with exact full filled/pending quantity. It requires a **unique** exact matching ordinary order-book child row.
- Verifies one or more unique individual BUY trade IDs, strict side BUY, exact parent/exchange reference, option instrument and native product, positive integer quantities, positive numeric paise-compatible prices, no overflow, and total individual executed quantity equaling original full BUY. The quantity-weighted BUY trade price must agree with broker order average and original durable BUY entry price to at most one paise. A second unrelated same-option SELL is not misinterpreted as an extra BUY; this is a per-original-child receipt, **not full account-wide order reconciliation**.
- Read-only `fetchOriginalBuyTradeEvidenceReadOnly()` calls existing Upstox `getOrderDetails`, `getOrderTrades`, `getOrderBook` methods. Any missing endpoint, malformed data, daily history truncation or transport failure is SOURCE UNAVAILABLE or CONFLICT—not proof the original BUY was absent or that the old BUY was filled.
- SQLite **Migration 021** adds immutable `ema_entry_buy_receipts` and unique `ema_entry_buy_trade_receipts` tables. A single `dbEngine.appendEmaOriginalBuyTradeEvidence()` **IMMEDIATE transaction** rechecks the current local long position, exact account/product/instrument, attempt ID, original broker BUY child, full BUY attempt quantity, original whole lots, recorded entry price and BUY execution volume. It inserts the complete receipt and all trade IDs once, as a unit. Identical repeat capture is idempotent; any changed trade ID quantity/price/exchange reference, header/account/product/order mismatch, missing prior trade, overfill, altered original entry journal or invalid price/quantity returns fixed conflict codes without partial write.
- New SUPERADMIN-only `POST /api/ema15m/original-buy-evidence-capture` accepts `{positionId, expectedAccountId}`, verifies authenticated connected Upstox account and uniquely matching local open/recoverable option position/original fully FILLED BUY attempt **before** GET-only broker reads. Successful valid evidence is written **only to local private SQLite**. Response provides only redacted fixed reason codes, number of stored BUY trades and hardcoded `automaticClosureAuthorized:false`, `realisedPnlWriteAuthorized:false`, `independentExchangeReceiptCertified:false`, `liveNrmlBuyAuthorized:false`, `brokerWrites:0`. No account numbers, order/venue/trade IDs, instrument keys, prices, tokens, BUY/SELL/GTT placement, local position CLOSE or P&L mutation.
- **23 new synthetic/actual SQLite tests** cover full 2x50 BUY weighted price, NRML D vs MIS I, missing/multiple order-book records, missing trade GETs, partial entry despite local FILLED, wrong child/account/contract/exchange ID, wrong product/side, unsafe trade IDs/quantities/prices, altered weighted price, other later same-option SELL, immutable and identical trade replay, atomic DB conflicts and unchanged position/P&L. Previous targeted Phase79–100 tests expected **375**; Phase101 targets **398 synthetic/SQLite tests**, subject to actual CI logs and corrections.

## What this does NOT prove

`BROKER_REPORTED_ORIGINAL_BUY_ONLY_NOT_CERTIFIED` explicitly means **broker-reported provenance**, not an independently authenticated exchange fill, complete P&L, tax calculation, linked broker fees, no other account BUY/SELL/GTT, or permission to close a live option. Even when both BUY and SELL receipts are consistent, the snapshots and source APIs are not atomic, missing older-day broker history can be unavoidable, and full account-wide capital/carry/expiry/risk reconciliation is not yet implemented. Phase100 preview's `originalBuyTradeIdsIndependentlyReconciled:false` **remains unchanged** until separate independent venue evidence and a formal lifecycle release. No verified net realised P&L, no broker charge assumptions, no automatic position closure.
